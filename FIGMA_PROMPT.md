## Grid and Composition

Desktop:
- Use a 12-column grid.
- Use asymmetry with disciplined alignment.
- Use generous negative space.
- Use thin rules and numbered sections.
- Allow content to cross or span columns intentionally.
- Avoid a repeated card component as the main composition.

Mobile:
- Redesign the hierarchy.
- Keep project evidence visible.
- Convert hover-only content into always-visible content.
- Maintain strong typography and rhythm.
- Avoid tiny metadata and dense diagrams.

## Color

Start with:
- Warm off-white paper background
- Near-black ink
- Muted graphite secondary text
- One signal accent such as cobalt, safety orange, or deep red
- Fine low-contrast rules

Provide accessible color styles and state combinations.

Do not use a gradient as the identity.

## Typography

Create a two-family system:
- A strong grotesk or humanist sans for body and interface
- A serif or mono accent for editorial or technical metadata

Use no more than three families.

Explain every font choice.

Make the type scale responsive and practical. Do not make the hero so large that the name becomes unreadable on mobile.

## Imagery and Graphic Language

Do not use stock photos or AI-generated illustrations.

Use:
- Custom system diagrams
- Inline SVG architecture fragments
- Fine technical labels
- Registration marks
- Small project numbers
- Carefully cropped screenshots only when real screenshots are supplied later
- A subtle paper or print texture

Create placeholder frames for real project screenshots, clearly marked for replacement. Do not fabricate product screenshots.
